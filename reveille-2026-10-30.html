The opposition faces a tricky choice: attempt to form a unity government with Netanyahu, after channeling anger at the prime minister during the campaign, or partner with the Israeli-Arab parties, which performed unusually well in the election. The latter option, however, is deeply unpalatable to most of the Jewish opposition. A period of negotiations now commences, while Netanyahu remains in a caretaker position."

Washington Should Not Push Europe Back Toward Russian Gas

"Russian President Vladimir Putin wants to recover the revenue Russia lost from its decline in gas exports to Europe. In a reversal of longstanding U.S. policy, Washington is trying to help him. At the October 27 Gas Exporting Countries Forum summit in Moscow, Putin criticized Western sanctions and pitched Russia as a solution to rising energy prices. This comes as U.S. officials are reportedly pushing Europe to revive imports of Russian gas following a potential agreement to end the Ukraine war. Doing so would help fuel Russian rearmament and reverse the progress Europe has made in reducing its dependence on Russian gas."
