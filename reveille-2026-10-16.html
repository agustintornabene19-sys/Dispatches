The Next War of Attrition Is in Yemen

"Battlefield dynamics in Yemen shift quickly, but Houthi aims do not. A movement organized around radical ideology and perpetual conflict now shares a border with a state organized around modernization and prosperity. Saudi Arabia and the Houthis both have formidable but very different military capabilities, and neither can force a quick decision on the other. That is the recipe for a long war of attrition. The closest precedent is the war in Ukraine, to which the Yemen war is increasingly linked... Iran and the Houthis are both betting that the Saudis are sufficiently anxious to hold on to their peaceful model of business, tourism, energy and investment to settle at almost any price and end the present conflict.

The Houthi ideological program is anti-Saudi, anti-American, anti-Israel, anti-democratic, anti-liberal and antisemitic. It has long cast the Saudi monarchy as an American-Israeli puppet unworthy of the title Custodian of the Two Holy Mosques."

Why Are EU Elites So Slavishly Devoted to the USA?

"At a structural level, NATO 3.0 is a collaborative survival strategy managed by a transatlantic functional elite... both U.S. and European ruling strata recognize that their shared global leverage — the financial, technological, logistical, and military levers that make up the multi-layered cage — is under severe strain due to global shifts. For European functional elites, tying their states to the U.S. command layer is seen as the only viable mechanism to preserve the broader transatlantic capitalist order from which their own position and wealth derive.
