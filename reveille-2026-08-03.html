The Latinos that could unlock the majority
"Democratic Latino strategists are trying to clue their party in on an overlooked voter that could unlock a Democratic majority — and it cuts through the cycle’s biggest races: Pay attention to Puerto Ricans... 'You’re talking about a set of Puerto Rican voters that literally alone potentially flip which party controls Congress,' Rafael Collazo, UnidosUS Action Fund’s executive director, told Playbook.

Puerto Ricans living in the mainland U.S. have a long history of voting blue, helping send Democrats like Reps. Darren Soto in Central Florida or Nydia Velázquez in New York City to Congress. But many of them, especially young Puerto Rican men, joined the overwhelming rightward shift of Latinos toward Donald Trump and Republicans in 2024. Then in 2025, another shift: some Puerto Ricans who had backed Republicans switched their votes again, this time backing Democrat Mikie Sherrill for governor in New Jersey."
