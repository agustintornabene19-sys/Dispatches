Why Iran-Contra still matters [more · less · never again]

UnHerd · Christian Parenti

On Nov. 3, 1986, almost exactly 40 years ago, a Lebanese magazine called Ash Shiraa reported that the United States, with Israel as intermediary, had clandestinely sold millions of dollars of anti-aircraft missiles to Ayatollah Khomeini's Iran in exchange for the release of six American hostages held by Hezbollah in Lebanon. It was one of a cascade of revelations about the covert workings of the American state that came to be known as the Iran-Contra affair. Forty years on, the state too often operates along similar lines, but for a rare moment in the late 1980s, there was real accountability.
