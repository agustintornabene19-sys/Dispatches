Trump's Blob

On how the foreign-policy establishment absorbed MAGA rather than the other way around.

"During the Obama administration, Deputy National Security Adviser Ben Rhodes—laboring to win approval for a diplomatic agreement with Iran—named the network of think tanks, government advisers, profiteers, industrialists, and Congresspeople who make up the American foreign policy establishment 'the Blob.' And what has become of the Blob during the Trump administration? 'At the institutions that certify foreign policy expertise,' Jonathan Guyer writes, 'MAGA operatives seem to be displacing the traditional diplomatic corps.' American bellicosity remains the order of the day, but there is now a 'brash and pugilistic' approach to world affairs. No more 'talk about winning hearts and minds or carrying liberties to unfree quarters of the globe; this is about raw American power, an attitude exemplified by the Jerusalem cross—the emblem of the Crusades—tattooed on [Pete] Hegseth's chest.'"

Hezbollah Explosives Debunk Lebanon's Sovereignty Myth

On a truckload of C-4 and the farce of a stalled investigation.

"The Republic of Lebanon operates under a dangerous illusion of authority. In reality, the state fears Hezbollah, abides by its diktats, and actively dismantles its own rule of law to accommodate the Iranian proxy... This latest crisis began with a routine checkpoint stop in the mountain town of Dahr al-Baydar. Lebanon's Internal Security Forces stopped two Hezbollah operatives driving a truck loaded with 500 kilograms of military-grade C-4 and TNT. Rather than distance itself from the shipment, Hezbollah lawmakers publicly claimed the explosives and demanded their immediate return. Hezbollah's brazen demand directly violates Lebanese law... Under interrogation, the arrested Hezbollah operatives admitted that a man known as 'Hajj Sajjed' had given them the explosives to deliver to 'Hajj Zolfikar' in Beirut's southern suburbs. The judiciary issued arrest warrants for the individuals behind these aliases, but the investigation stalled under the convenient pretext that investigators could not identify the men behind the pseudonyms. The state's feigned ignorance is a farce."

— Hussain Abdul-Hussain, This Is Beirut, via FDD · Read › [more · less · never again]
