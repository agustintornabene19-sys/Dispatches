Pragmatism Could Draw Syria and Hezbollah Into an Accommodation

"During the Syrian civil war, Hezbollah spent years fighting the factions now ruling Damascus. Now, even as Damascus intercepts the group's weapons and disrupts its supply routes, Hezbollah's Secretary General Naim Qassem is courting Syria's new rulers. In a televised speech on August 4, Qassem expressed his willingness to meet with Syria's new leadership, emphasizing 'the importance and necessity of a fraternal, positive, and cooperative relationship between Lebanon and Syria.'

The feeling is mutual. In June, Syrian President Ahmad al-Sharaa said he was prepared to 'sit at the same table with Hezbollah if it serves the interests of Syria and Lebanon,' effectively rejecting President Donald Trump's request for Damascus to play a larger role in disarming Hezbollah. Hezbollah welcomed that decision, opening the door for a potential pragmatic relationship between the two adversaries."
