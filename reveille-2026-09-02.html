The Axis of Aggressors Is Getting Ready for War. Is America?

"America has grown addicted to the phrase 'wars of choice,' often using it to vilify the U.S. government for campaigns deemed unnecessary. Wholesale skepticism of military power, however, undermines Washington's ability to defend the homeland against surprise attacks like Pearl Harbor and 9/11. America might not choose war, but war will likely choose America, at home and around the world.

In 'Axis of Aggressors,' my Foundation for Defense of Democracies colleagues Brad Bowman, Elaine Dezenski, and Mark Montgomery examine how China, Russia, Iran, and North Korea cooperate across multiple domains. These regimes supplement each other's military production while building parallel financial networks to bypass Western economic tools. Together, their economic weight approaches a quarter of global GDP.

A primary engine of this collaboration is a shadow economic system engineered to evade sanctions. Jurisdictions such as Malaysia and Turkey play structurally distinct but mutually reinforcing roles in sustaining Axis trade. In global energy markets, Malaysia has emerged as a key relabeling hub for Iranian oil, with Chinese customs data showing imports from Malaysia far exceeding the country's production. China reported importing 1.3 million barrels per day of 'Malaysian' oil in 2025, more than twice Malaysia's total daily production of 535,000 barrels in 2024."

— Hussain Abdul-Hussain, Foundation for Defense of Democracies / This Is Beirut · Read › [more · less · never again]

Qusra Under Siege

"Last Saturday, in the West Bank town of Jalud, a group of Israeli settlers armed with clubs beat, unprovoked, three members of an NBC news team and the Palestinian woman they were interviewing. This kind of violence... is routine in the Palestinian West Bank. About two miles northeast of Jalud, in the town of Qusra, the settlers have been engaged in a monthslong campaign of bloody intimidation: On July 26 settlers burned down the al-Rahma mosque in the village. Armed settlers brutally attacked Palestinian farmers from the village and the Israeli and international activists who were trying to protect them; several were severely wounded. In March twenty-eight-year-old Amir Moatasem Odeh was killed by a settler; another stabbed his father.

The settler assassins were arrested by the police and as usual released within a few hours. No action has been taken against them. Settlers are above and beyond the law... the orders come from the settlers, though just behind them sits the nefarious prime minister, intent on destruction, and the mindless ministers of his government."

A 1931 field problem, still worth solving

"The following problem appeared in the first semi-annual volume of the Infantry School Mailing List, which rolled off the press in 1931... 'You may hide in the caves, They'll be only your graves, But you can't get away from the guns.' — Rudyard Kipling, Screw Guns.

SITUATION: A Blue regiment, an interior unit, is to attack southward... (1) The Blues are well-trained veterans, supported by one battalion of light artillery, but no tanks. (2) The Blues are partly-trained troops in their first action, led by recently commissioned officers for the most part. A company of tanks is attached to the regiment, and supporting artillery behind it is lined up almost hub-to-hub. Blue artillery and aviation are considerably superior to the Reds.

REQUIREMENT: Where does the regiment make its main effort in each case?"
