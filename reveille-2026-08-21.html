From the Branch

Machine Gun Man (II)

"In the middle of November 1916, after the breakthrough of the Transylvanian Alps, the German Ninth Army of General von Falkenhayn was advancing victoriously through Wallachia... The opening of this defile was a matter of life and death for the supply of the army. Therefore the General von Falkenhayn decided to intrust this mission to a small independent detachment. This meant for it, and its leader, great responsibility, daring, and judgement. It was one of the few opportunities ever granted to a reinforced battalion to operate independently. At dawn, 20 November 1916, the detachment was assembled north of Filiasul. Commanded by Captain Picht, it consisted of the 1st Battalion, 148th Infantry; two truck-borne anti-aircraft guns of the 1st Battery, 79th Field Artillery; one-third of an ammunition train of the 79th Field Artillery; one officers' patrol of the 10th Dragoons; parts of the motor transport unit of the 41st Infantry Division; and one radio detachment."

— Bruce Ivar Gudmundsson, The Tactical Notebook · Read › [more · less · never again]
