From the Branch

Machine Gun Man (IV)

Bruce Ivar Gudmundsson · The Tactical Notebook

"On 6 October 1936, the Army High Command (Oberkommando des Heeres) published a graphical catalog of deployable units serving in the German Army on that date... Two battalions, however, stood out from this otherwise conventional crowd. Styled as 'motorized machine gun battalions,' these outliers provided muscle-powered army corps with mobile reserves of rifle-caliber firepower. To that end, each battalion of that type consisted of a headquarters, a communications platoon, a motorcycle platoon, and three fully motorized machine gun companies. (Each of the latter broke down into four four-piece platoons, each of which employed four heavy machine guns.)"
