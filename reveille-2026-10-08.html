More than a century later, armies are confronting an unsettling echo of that experience in Ukraine.

The Mecca Pact Won't Deter Yemen's Houthis
"Less than two months after its signing, Pakistan, Turkey, and Saudi Arabia activated the Mecca Pact for the first time. On October 5, during an emergency meeting held in Riyadh, the three countries agreed 'to move immediately to the practical implementation of the collective defense commitments and to take the necessary measures to provide the agreed military forces and capabilities and ensure their rapid deployment in the kingdom.' The decision followed the launch of a counteroffensive by the Yemeni government's forces, backed by Saudi Arabia, against the Iran-backed Houthis. The offensive aims to retake strategic territory along Yemen's western coast that the Houthis seized in recent weeks, including areas overlooking the Bab al-Mandeb Strait.

The pact's activation is significant at a time when the Houthis have targeted Mecca and Medina, Islam's two holiest cities. The more important test, however, is how far Pakistan and Turkey are willing to go and whether they will stick to defensive measures, especially given the political and strategic constraints that may limit how directly they are prepared to confront the Houthi..."
