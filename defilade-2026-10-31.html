War on the Rocks on AI-generated courses of action and the staff process.

At Nellis Air Force Base last year, an AI system was tasked with building courses of action, a complex operational problem that military planners have spent generations learning to perform. The AI system took less than a minute to complete its task with solutions rated at 97 percent viable and tactically valid (essentially, whether the proposed plan could work given operational constraints such as fuel and force composition). By contrast, their human counterparts took roughly 19 minutes and only reached 48 percent viability.

Review of Beyond Checks and Balances: The Political Purpose of the Separation of Powers.

Defenders of the separation of powers often understand it as purposefully obstructing or impeding government for the sake of individual rights. Some measure of gridlock and inefficiency is the price we pay to avert a too powerful and intrusive state; and when the branches come into conflict, the Supreme Court will sort it all out. Meanwhile, critics of the separation of powers frequently regard it as an eighteenth-century anachronism that frustrates the more activist and regulatory government needed in modern times. The essays collected in Beyond Checks and Balances: The Political Purpose of the Separation of Powers , though, differ from both of these views.
