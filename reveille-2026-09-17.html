"At a high level, USG should respond to AI by giving up on its faux 18th-century nonsense hat-and-musket larp ideology and all its consequent paperwork, deciding today is the first day of the rest of its life, and hiring a good king. If this task seems too much for us, idk. Try a CEO search firm? Since, due to Satan's many snares and wiles, this is not about to happen, let's stick to describing how a national CEO would respond to the AI crisis (it is a crisis!) right now."

"AI is a tool. It is not magic. AI does not act. Humans act, with the power of our tools. The power of this tool has not even created new risks. It has made old risks, which we should have addressed long ago, unacceptably urgent. The first risk is cybersecurity... The second risk is biosecurity. The USG must establish and maintain a biosecurity filter which all DNA synthesis must pass. It does not just 'grep for smallpox,' but finds anything like a viral motif. The filter is automated and runs in less than a minute, in a secure enclave which does not save or export data."
