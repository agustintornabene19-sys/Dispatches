From the Branch

Thinking Fast and Thinking Slow.

"Gerhard von Scharnhorst (1755-1813) spent the first nine years of his military career in two very different institutions. From the summer of 1773 to the summer of 1778... he studied at the Wilhelmstein, the military academy of Count William of Schaumburg-Lippe Bückeburg. From 1778 through the summer of 1782, he served as a junior officer in the 8th Cavalry Regiment ('d'Estorff's Dragoons') of the Hanoverian Army."

"Each of these two periods exercised a powerful influence in the education of the young soldier. Nonetheless, biographers of Scharnhorst have invariably paid far more attention to the five years that Scharnhorst spent at the Wilhelmstein than to his four years as a dragoon."

— Bruce Ivar Gudmundsson, The Tactical Notebook  |  Read › [more · less · never again]
