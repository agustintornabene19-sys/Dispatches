Aftershocks: Great Powers and Domestic Reforms in the Twentieth Century

"'One thing about which fish know exactly nothing is water, since they have no anti-environment which would enable them to perceive the element they live in' — Marshall McLuhan

If left to its own devices, much of Europe would fall away from liberal democracy in due time and segue (or revert) into other forms of governance... In the Interwar Period, Europe had largely given up on democracy. Portugal, Italy, Hungary, Germany, Yugoslavia, Poland, and others became dictatorships. Spain collapsed into a civil war... All of these countries (including the Yugoslav successor states) today are liberal democracies. So ingrained is liberal democracy in their states that one finds it difficult to imagine them being any other way. The fact of the matter is that their democratic pedigrees are quite new and thin, and my argument is that absent US domination over the continent — and if permitted to arrange themselves as they see fit — they would wither away."

— Niccolo Soldo, Fisted by Foucault
