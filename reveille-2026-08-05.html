Cossack or Uhlan?

"The Cossack shocks the sentimental idea of cavalry. The glitter and the tinsel that comes from old days of joust and tourney are not in his line. He wears not a button or a bright thing about him, and he rides his little plug of a horse holding his knees almost as high as his chin. His seat resembles that of a monkey in a circus race. The haute école and the manége he regards as mere cavalry quackery... German cavalry is the pride of the race; officered entirely from the nobility... Filled with the romance and traditions of a glorious history, it need not be doubted that it has all the confidence of those hard-headed nobles who so freely gave their lives for an idea at Crécy and Morgarten... Germans point to great results and say that by such should their methods be judged, but they ignore the fact that they have only encountered inferior representatives of the same cavalry school with themselves."

China is Building a Blue Granary

"Off the coast of Zhejiang province, an unusual vessel plies the East China Sea. It could be mistaken for a cargo ship, but there are no containers on its deck. Its track is strange: instead of running between sequential ports of call, it bounces between points at sea for months at a time... The ship's transponder identifies it as the Guoxin 2-1. It is moving with the tidal currents, seeking water at the right temperature for the cargo in its hull: vast tanks of large yellow croaker, raised from fry in the ship's underbelly... Farther up the coast, off southern Shandong province, a crew in a small offshore boat works to sink a concrete block. Seagrass seeds have been tucked into nooks along its sides.
