How Trump Redefined U.S.-Iran Policy At The UN

"Candor and clarity of purpose are not themes traditionally associated with United Nations General Assembly meetings in New York. The annual summit... has long been a preferred venue for dictators and kleptocrats to make jargon-laden national — or, quite often, personal — cases against the West... Not so with President Trump. And that may be a good thing. Despite occasional references to domestic matters — by now a hallmark of almost any Trump speech — his address offered remarkable clarity and candor on Iran policy, casting the issue in zero-sum terms. Trump outlined his multiple attempts to negotiate with the clerical regime over its nuclear program, all to no avail. Each time, the regime's spurning, bad-faith engagement, foot-dragging, or delay brought about a fate that could have been avoided. Put differently, the Islamic Republic is the main force pushing Trump to embrace the role of Commander in Chief every time he tries to act as Dealmaker in Chief."
