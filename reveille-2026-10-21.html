Measuring the Swarm Economy

"In early September 2026, OpenAI set up a team to solve the Navier-Stokes Millennium Prize problem, a famously difficult open problem in mathematics. In the span of 88 hours, a solution was achieved by a set of over ten thousand agents operating in parallel and coordinating their behavior. They sent 2.7 million messages to one another and used around 130 billion output tokens—enough data to fill a million books. The AI-formulated Navier-Stokes 'forced blowup' proof was recognized as an unexpected great leap forward for mathematics.

Fewer observers recognized how much of a leap forward OpenAI's massive multi-agent effort represented for the artificial intelligence industry. As the AI industry wades into these uncharted waters, it will need to develop a new type of benchmarking that evaluates models' ability to organize, engage in cooperative behaviors, and form interdependent networks."

— Gavin Morrison, Palladium Magazine · Read › [more · less · never again]

How Russia Is Eclipsing the U.S. in Africa

"The Kremlin is preparing to showcase its steadily growing influence in Africa, which has often come at Washington's expense. On October 28-29, Moscow will host more than 30 African delegations for the third Russia-Africa Summit. More than four years after Russia's full-scale invasion of Ukraine, it is clear that U.S. sanctions and diplomacy have not isolated Russia in Africa. Instead, Moscow has expanded its political, economic, and military relationships across the continent, weakening the fight against terrorism, undermining U.S. efforts to end the war in Ukraine, and locking up critical minerals Washington needs.

This change for the worse was not inevitable. Washington's lackluster and incomplete efforts are partly to blame. To reverse the trend, the United States needs to step up and compete with Russia in Africa by imposing sanctions on the infrastructure Russia has built on the continent to facilitate sanctions evasion; extending the African Growth and Opportunity Act (AGOA)... making plain to African governments that hosting Russian forces on the Red Sea or in the Indian Ocean... will cost them Western assistance and investment; and filling vacant ambassadorships."
