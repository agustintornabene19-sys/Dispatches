The Right Argues With Itself

Handoverism considered retarded [more · less · never again]

Curtis Yarvin (Gray Mirror) · ~15 min

A reply to Scott Alexander's case for handing government over to AI agents.

The skyhook is that agents can act and have power. No. Only humans act. Agents are tools—like a bulldozer. Agents will never have power. Agents will always be under human control. A bulldozer can do bad things, but the actor is the bulldozer driver. Smarter models do not make this skyhook more true. They make it less true. Human control over agents is increasing, not decreasing.
