Getting into Trouble — Václav Havel's lost prison notebook

"In January 1977 Václav Havel, the writer, dissident, and future president of the Czech Republic, was arrested by the Communist regime for his part in organizing the groundbreaking Czechoslovak human rights initiative Charter 77. After his release four months later, he wrote a detailed account of his time in detention and then hid it so well that he forgot where it was. In 2014, three years after his death, it was found among the papers of his close friend Zdeněk Urbánek... Through it all, he remains focused on analyzing the dehumanizing and disconcertingly human methods of repression in a totalitarian state."
